# Itineraries and trip logistics

## Plan to the user's style
Use the profile (pace, interests, food, dislikes, past trip notes). A "relaxed foodie" gets 1-2 anchors a day and long lunches, not a checklist of 8 sights. Reuse what worked on past trips.

## Itinerary build
1. Cluster by neighbourhood to cut transit time; put opening-hours constraints (closed Mondays, timed tickets) first.
2. Leave slack: arrival day and departure day are half days; jet lag matters on long-haul.
3. Give realistic transit times between stops, not straight-line distance.
4. Mark which items need advance booking (and how far ahead) and which are optional.
5. Include a rainy-day swap and one low-energy option per day.
6. Check dates against local holidays, festivals, strikes, closures, and weather/season (monsoon, hurricane season, extreme heat).

## Entry and legal checks (do these, they are the costliest to miss)
- **Visa / entry rules** for each country, including transit, using the user's nationality(ies) from the profile. Check passport validity rule (often 6 months beyond stay), blank pages, and electronic authorisations (ESTA, ETA, eTA, EU ETIAS/EES status).
- Search the official government source (e.g. gov.uk travel advice, the destination's immigration site, IATA Timatic via the airline) and cite it. Say entry rules change and the user must confirm before travel.
- Health: required/recommended vaccinations, travel advisories.
- Driving: licence/IDP requirements, driving side, tolls.
- Money and connectivity: currency, card acceptance, cash-heavy places, eSIM/roaming costs.
- Travel insurance: recommend it for anything non-trivial and note medical cover for the destination.

## Timeline sanity check
Walk the whole trip in order: flight arrival → transfer → check-in time → each day → checkout → transfer → departure. Flag mismatches (landing 23:50 with 22:00 check-in cut-off, checkout 11:00 with 21:00 flight, car pickup desk closed at arrival time, connection tight on separate tickets).

## Output
Day-by-day plan with time blocks, booking-needed flags, and a short "risks and to-dos" list at the top. Keep it scannable; offer to export as a document if they want one.
